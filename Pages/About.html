About SignSpeak

Breaking communication barriers through real-time hand sign detection technology.

What is SignSpeak?

SignSpeak is a real-time hand sign detection website designed to recognize hand signs using a camera and convert them into meaningful text. The system aims to make communication easier and more accessible for people who use hand signs to communicate.

Our Goal

Our goal is to build a simple and accessible system that can understand hand signs in real time. SignSpeak focuses on reducing communication barriers by providing a technology-based way to interpret hand signs.

Real-Time Detection

SignSpeak uses a camera to capture hand movements. The captured video is processed by the detection system, which identifies the corresponding hand sign and displays the detected result on the screen.

Simple & Easy to Use

The interface is designed to be simple. Users can open their camera, show a hand sign, and view the detected sign without complicated controls or setup.

How SignSpeak Works

1

Open Camera

Start the camera and allow the browser to access it.

2

Show Hand Sign

Place your hand in front of the camera and perform a supported hand sign.

3

Detect Sign

The system processes the camera feed and identifies the detected hand sign.

4

View Result

The detected sign is displayed on the screen in real time.
